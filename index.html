Company overview

Corporate information, clearly presented.

Next Generation Management Corp. is a Nevada-incorporated public company identified on OTC Markets under the trading symbol NGMC.

The Company operates an online retail platform through a wholly owned subsidiary that markets and sells consumer products through Amazon, Etsy, and other digital retail channels. The Company also identifies Fundraising.com as a subsidiary. The business includes associated intellectual property, customer lists, digital infrastructure, vendor relationships, and operational systems supporting commercial activity.

According to company-provided information, following its acquisition of the operating business, the Company has continued processing customer transactions and maintaining vendor and service-provider relationships in the ordinary course of business. The Company describes these activities as revenue-generating.
